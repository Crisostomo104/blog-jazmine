Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Welcome to Jazmine's Journal! Your privacy and digital security are incredibly important to me. I believe in a clean, transparent, and worry-free browsing experience.

Zero Data Collection

Because Jazmine's Journal operates as a completely static, informational blog, we do not collect, store, or process any personal data from our visitors. There are no tracking cookies, no background analytics, no hidden user accounts, and no forms that secretly capture your information.

Safe Browsing

You are completely free to browse, read, and enjoy all the articles on this website with total peace of mind. Since we don't ask for any personal details, there is absolutely zero risk of your data being sold or mishandled.

If you wish to reach out, you can do so directly via the social platforms linked on my Let's Connect page. Any messages sent to me through those platforms are governed by their respective privacy policies.
